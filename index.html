Field Guide

↑

Jump

Space / W / ↑ · hold for a taller hop

Pip’s first hop. A short coyote window still lets you jump just after walking off a ledge.

↑↑

Double jump

tap jump again in the air · one extra hop

Uses your air jump. Landing, a mushroom spring, or a stomp gives it back.

↝

Glide

hold jump while falling · max fall is slowed

After the hop peaks, keep holding Space to float. Great for wide pits. Works on or off the skateboard (the board is a bit heavier).

🛹

Skateboard

touch the board to pick it up · S or ↓ to hop on/off

Ride for extra speed and a long slide. You can still jump, double jump, glide, and shoot. Getting hit knocks you off — you keep the board. Falling in a pit still ends the run.

✦

Star blaster

aim with the mouse · click or J / K / X / Z · 1 damage

Every gun in Cozy-Platformer fires toward the cursor — the Star Blaster is the one you have now. Unlimited pellets. Sproutlings take 2 hits. Puff-bats take 1. Pellets pop on walls.

↓

Stomp

land on a critter’s head · bounce + air jump refill

Works on both meadow pests. Safer than a hug. Touching them from the side costs 1 heart.

Sproutling

HP 2 · contact 1 heart · stomp: yes · shots: 2

A grumpy walking turnip. Patrols flat ground and turns around at ledges and walls. Does not jump or shoot. Best dealt with a stomp or two pellets.

Puff-bat

HP 1 · contact 1 heart · stomp: yes · shots: 1

A cream puff with wings. Hovers in a slow sine wave and drifts back and forth. Soft, but still costs a heart if you walk into it. One pellet or one stomp pops it.

💧

Dewdrop

HP 1 · contact 1 heart · Brook Bend

A hovering water puff. Real dew forms when night air cools and water vapor condenses on leaves. One pellet or a stomp pops it.

🍃

Leaf-mite

HP 2 · contact 1 heart · faster patrol · Canopy Climb

A speedy leaf critter. Real mites and springtails help shred fallen leaves so nutrients go back into the soil. Two shots or a stomp.

↑

Sand-hopper

HP 2 · hops · Dune Drift

A bouncing desert pest. Real desert locusts and kangaroo rats save energy with hops. Two shots or a stomp — watch the jump.

🦀

Pebble crab

HP 2 · contact 1 heart · Tide Pool

Side-scuttles the rocks. Real shore crabs hide in tide pools at low tide and come out to graze algae when the sea returns.

🌙

Orchard moth

HP 1 · flies · Night Orchard

A night pollinator puff. Many moths find pale flowers by scent. One pellet or a stomp. Please imagine it as a pest stand-in, not a real moth to squash.

🦇

Cave bat

HP 1 · flies in the dark · Cave Glow

A click-and-listen flyer. Real bats use echolocation — they shout tiny sounds and steer by the echo. One pellet or a stomp.

⚡

Storm spark

HP 1 · Prairie Storm

A charged puff that hangs in the gusts. Real lightning is a giant spark; thunder is the air slamming shut after it.

🫧

Peat spore

HP 1 · drifts · Bog Lantern

A slow moss puff. Real peatlands hold half-rotted plants for thousands of years because the water is too sour and cold for full decay.

🔥

Ember hopper

HP 2 · hops · Volcano Rim

A hot bounce on new rock. Real lava is melted stone; once it cools it can become the soil of a future garden. Do not stand in the orange.

✦

Aurora wisp

HP 1 · flies · Aurora Ridge

A charged night puff. Real auroras are gases glowing after the solar wind hits Earth’s magnetic shield — not fire in the sky.

Pip (you)

HP 3 hearts · pellet 1 dmg · fall = instant out

Beanie ranger of Meadow Mile. Hearts only drop when a critter bumps you (not from stomps). Falling into a pit or off the map is a full knockout — no checkpoint save.

Grass & dirt

solid ground · cannot collect

The meadow floor. Stand on it. Dirt under grass is just filler. Walking off it into empty air is a pit.

Brick

solid · bump from below to smash

Jump up into a brick from underneath to break it. You do not pick bricks up. Useful for opening a path or reaching a star above.

Honey block

solid · bump from below → star · then becomes used

The gold block with a star stamp. Hit it with your head. A star pops out and you collect it. After that it turns brown and stays put — empty, still solid.

Wood ledge

one-way platform · jump up through it

Land on it from above. You can hop up through it from below. Some of them slide (the moving island over the late pit).

Sand

solid · extra slide · Dune Drift

Sand grains are leftover rock. You can stand on it, but it is slippery. Wind above the dunes will also push you.

Mud

solid · slow and sticky · Bog Lantern

Waterlogged peat. You can walk it, but it grabs your boots. Real bogs stay wet because sphagnum moss holds water like a sponge.

Lava

instant out · Volcano Rim

Melted rock. Touching it ends the run. Hop the cooled stone. When lava hardens it writes brand-new land.

Ice

solid · very slippery · Alpine Pass

Packed snow that froze again. You can stand on it, but friction is tiny — skate or take short steps. Real glaciers are rivers of this ice.

Cactus

hurts on touch · not collectible

A water-saving plant with a waxy skin and spines. In Cozy-Platformer the spines cost a heart. Hop over; do not hug.

Garden tube

solid obstacle · cannot enter

Terracotta chimneys. Walk around or hop over. They do not warp anywhere in this mile.

Mushroom spring

land on it · big bounce · refills air jump

Pink cap, cream spots. Step on it to launch. Hold jump after the bounce if you want to glide the extra height.

Garden flag

touch to clear the mile

The finish of every habitat. Walk into the red banner to stamp your journal and open the next map.

Stars

touch to collect · also pop from honey blocks

Floating honey-gold tokens. Walk or jump into them. Bumping a honey block from below spits one out and you grab it automatically. They are the mile’s score — they are not spent on anything yet.

Hearts

3 max · lost on critter contact · not collected in the mile

Shown in the top-left. You start with a full set. There is no heart pickup on Meadow Mile — play carefully. A pit fall ignores leftover hearts and ends the run.

🛹

Skateboard pickup

touch the parked board · then S / ↓ to ride

One sits near the start of the mile, another later on the high garden. Touch it once to keep it for the rest of the run. It is not a block and cannot be stored in a bag — you just own it.
