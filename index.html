Hired hands

They work so you don't have to. You pay a wage; the monger pays it back when they sell. Crew keeps a 1–5% penny profit. Your own hands keep 4–20%.

Hand work pays 4–20% pennies on bait. Caster, reeler, and monger together keep 1–5% after wages. Always a little, never a loss.

What you're angling for

You name a fish. The water answers with a table, not a promise. Bait, rod, tide, moon, and how well you know them all lean the roll.

Bait tin

Casts spend bait. The right smell makes the named fish more likely. The wrong smell still feeds someone.

Rod rack

A heavier rod holds trophies. A kinder rod hurries the shy ones. The moon rod has opinions after dusk.

Traps

Pots and weirs work while you look the other way. They like the matching water.

Boat nook

Friends who fish while you rest. Charters leave at dawn and come home with a story.

Chandler's cart

Line, hook, icehouse, and a little tide-magic.

Creel

Tap a thing to sell one. Trophies keep their name in the book even after the pan.

Recipes

Cooking turns patience into something warmer. A bigger kitchen unlocks the feast recipes.

Smokehouse

Hang a fish. Come back when the rafters have finished the sentence.

Livewell

A few fish stay. They pay rent in pearls, fry, and a quieter dock.

Neighbors

Bring what they asked. Hearts open slowly. Five hearts unlock a real perk. Chat changes as they learn your step.

Harbor board

Wren pins two kindnesses. They reset with the week. Filling one also writes your name kinder.

Peddler's crate

Every few days a crate arrives. It does not wait long.

Dock goods

Pretty things that quietly help. The bench is for sitting. The gull bath is for birds with opinions.

Almanac

Favors are how the week says thank you. Spend them on weather, stock, luck, or hurry.

Fish book

First catch, biggest, the ones that got away. A fish is known only after it has introduced itself.

Trophy wall

Letters and dusk

The harbor writes back, slowly.

Paragon tree

Fold a long harbor into stars. Spend them on gifts the next dock remembers. Prestige earns stars and restarts. Restart keeps the tree without new stars. Reset on Saves wipes the tree too.

Local harbors

Each profile is a separate dock on this computer. Switching saves the one you’re in first. Reset wipes this harbor and its paragon tree. Prestige and restart live under Paragon.
