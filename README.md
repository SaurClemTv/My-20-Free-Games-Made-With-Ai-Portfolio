# Cozy-Platformer

A cartoony 2D platformer in the old-school Mario tradition — run, hop, stomp — with modern jump feel and a star-blaster.

You play **Pip**, a beanie-wearing nature ranger. Three habitats:

1. **Meadow Mile** — grasslands, pollen, soil
2. **Brook Bend** — streams, the water cycle
3. **Canopy Climb** — forest layers and photosynthesis
4. **Dune Drift** — wind, sand, water-wise plants
5. **Tide Pool** — tides, salt, rocky-shore life
6. **Night Orchard** — moon, moths, fruit, stars
7. **Cave Glow** — limestone caves, stalactites, echolocation
8. **Prairie Storm** — weather, lightning, fire ecology
9. **Alpine Pass** — glaciers, thin air, snowpack
10. **Bog Lantern** — peat, sour water, carnivorous plants
11. **Volcano Rim** — lava, plates, geothermal heat
12. **Aurora Ridge** — solar wind, magnetic field, polar night

Walk into ranger signs and floating notes to fill the **Journal** tab of the Field Guide.

## Play

Open `index.html` — same as the other cozy games. Double-click it, or double-click `Play.bat`.

No server needed. Everything loads from the folder.

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Move | ← → or A D |
| Jump | Space, W, or ↑ (hold for higher; tap again in the air for a double jump) |
| Glide | Hold jump while falling |
| Skate | Touch the board, then S or ↓ to hop on/off |
| Aim | Move the mouse — every gun fires toward the cursor |
| Shoot | Click, or J / K / X / Z |
| Maps / Hub | Title menu · Maps button · Esc from a map result |
| Field Guide | G, or the book button |
| Pause | Esc |
| Restart | Enter or R after a win / fall |

Touch: top of the screen jumps, left/right thirds move, middle third shoots.

## Feel

Modern platformer juice on a classic stage shape:

- coyote time, jump buffering, and a double jump
- hold jump after the peak to glide
- pick up a skateboard and ride it (S / ↓)
- falling into a pit or off the map is a full death
- Field Guide (the book button) is a Pokédex of moves, critters, and blocks
- stomp **or** shoot the sprout-bugs and puff-bats
- bump honey blocks for stars, smash bricks from below
- mushroom springs, one-way wood, a moving island
- reach the garden flag to clear the mile
